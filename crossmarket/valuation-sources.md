# Valuation data pipeline
- The default TAIEX panel uses the Securities and Futures Bureau, Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) published **上市本益比** series. CSV from the [documented public dataset export](https://stat.fsc.gov.tw/api/v1/public/datasets/70284558-c657-47c0-9352-5e19bac25479/export), displayed by [金融競爭力重要資訊](https://stat.fsc.gov.tw/competitivenessinfo), under the FSC linked website statement and [Open Government Data License 1.0](https://data.gov.tw/license). The HTML adds a percent glyph; the export gives the literal market P/E multiple (e.g. 21.29 for Dec 2024, confirmed by the [Central Bank financial stability report](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/dl-216300-4799f610b1f540c7b97ce1f627d98bdf.html)), not 0.2129. 
- As of Nov 28 2026, the export has 238 raw rows, 121 distinct contiguous months from 2016-08 to 2026-08. Duplicate month values match and collapse to one. At the end of Nov 2026 the six-year view contains 69 of 72 possible months. Refresh via `python crossmarket/valuation-refresh-fsc.py` or pass a local copy of the export. This leaves source publication delays visible. 
- The Taiwan market P/E is not rebuilt from listed stocks and does not claim Nasdaq-comparable methodology. The river uses the exact matching month-end cash index close supplied via `crossmarket:closes` (or optionally user CSV) divided by the published market multiple. Quantile bands are retrospective and not a point-in-time model backtest.
- Nasdaq COMP / SOX and all three NTM FPE series are not published here. Published proprietary references are linked, not scraped/digitized/distributed. These series do not use ETF proxies, present-day estimate backfill, or the Nasdaq-100.
- Provide an entitled CSV (form `month,symbol,close,pe,fpe`) in the browser to show optional series. A local/offline utility `python crossmarket/valuation-import-c04001.py YOUR_REPORTS/*.zip > valuation-local.csv` with `xlrd` reads C04001 files lawfully acquired separately. Do not upload report files or this private output to the public repository without authorization.
- `node --test crossmarket/valuation.test.cjs` verifies 72-month slicing, reading a CSV with quoted prices and blanks, rejecting wrong index/missing and duplicate data, only same-month same-symbol joins and separate runs at gaps, and quantiles.
